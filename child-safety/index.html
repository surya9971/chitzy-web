Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Chitzy has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any such content or behaviour is strictly prohibited and leads to permanent removal and reporting to the authorities.

These standards apply to the Chitzy app — listed as “Chitzy” on Google Play and the Apple App Store (package com.chitzy.app), published by Chitzy™ — and to the Chitzy website at chitzy.in. They form part of our Terms of Service.

1. What is prohibited

Users must never use Chitzy to sexually abuse, exploit or endanger children. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Creating, sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind
  • Grooming — building trust with a minor for sexual purposes
  • Sexualising minors in any form, including in text, jokes or role-play
  • Sextortion — threatening or coercing a minor to obtain sexual content, money or anything else
  • Soliciting, arranging or promoting sexual contact with a minor, or child trafficking
  • Any other content or conduct that sexually exploits or endangers children

2. Who can use Chitzy

Chitzy is for adults only. Every user must be 18 or older, and must declare their age when signing up — the app does not allow sign-up below 18. Chitzy is rated 18+ on the App Store and Mature 18+ on Google Play. Accounts we identify as belonging to anyone under 18 are removed.

3. How Chitzy prevents abuse

  • Text only. Chitzy does not allow sending photos, videos or files — messages and posts are text only.
  • Moderation before delivery. Every message written in the app is checked by automated keyword filters and AI moderation before it is delivered. Messages that break our rules are blocked.
  • Curated web messages. People sending from the website can only choose from pre-written messages — they cannot type their own.
  • Blocking. Users can block anyone who contacts them. Blocks also apply to the sender's device, so new accounts on the same device stay blocked.

4. How to report

Every message in the Chitzy inbox has a report button — the red flag icon in the top-right corner of the message (shown once the message is unlocked), followed by “Report & Remove”. Reporting removes the message from your inbox immediately, blocks the sender from contacting you, and sends the report to the Chitzy team, who review reports within 24 hours.

To report anything else — a user, a safety concern or child safety — go to Profile → Report a concern in the app and choose “Child safety”. It is sent to the Chitzy team without leaving the app, and child safety reports are reviewed first.

You can also report any child safety concern directly to our child safety contact (see section 7). If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first.

5. How we respond to CSAE and CSAM

When we become aware of CSAE or CSAM on Chitzy, we:

  • Remove the content and permanently ban the account and its device
  • Preserve relevant information as required by law
  • Report it to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), and cooperate fully with law enforcement

6. Compliance with the law

Chitzy complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, as well as the laws of the other countries where Chitzy is available.
